### About me

I work as a fullstack developer at **Care of Carl**, a Swedish premium menswear e-commerce company.  I graduated from the Frontend Developer (React) programme at Yrkeshögskolan i Borås in 2026.

I care about interfaces that feel considered: clean design, sensible architecture, and code that's easy for the next person to read. Outside of work I like shipping small apps of my own — one of them is on Google Play.

- 🔭 **Currently:** e-commerce frontend, backend services at Care of Carl
- 🌱 **Learning:** deeper TypeScript, testing, and backend/database design with Supabase
- ⛳ **Off the keyboard:** golf and football
